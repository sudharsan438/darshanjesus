About the author

Stories that invite reflection.

Books that encourage readers to pause, question, feel, and step into a different perspective.

Darshan is an author and digital creator who explores the human mind, relationships, spirituality, karma, mystery, and the experiences that shape us.

His books span different genres and themes, but share one central intention: to help readers pause, reflect, and see familiar aspects of life from a deeper perspective.
